Mentaura Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Notice Version: v1.0 | SIH26094 Compliance

1. Purpose & Scope

Mentaura is a secure, case-aware support intelligence platform developed to assist victims, witnesses, and affected family members. This policy explains our data protection standards, role-based boundaries, and consent frameworks.

2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Information collected is strictly segregated according to verified platform roles. Selecting an official role during signup requires administrative review before any official access is granted. Victims, witnesses, and counsellors only have access to records permitted for their specific verified relationship.

3. Consent & Support Pulse Processing

Participation in Support Pulse check-ins is voluntary. Users have the right to select AI-assisted processing or direct Human Review Only for each check-in submission. All AI models operate as advisory support tools and require human oversight.

4. Data Minimisation & Security

Personal data is protected using role-based safeguards. For anonymous users, zero personal name, email, or phone number is stored. Session tokens are generated cryptographically and managed through secure HTTP cookies.

5. Contacting the Protection Unit

For privacy inquiries or verification questions, contact the District Support Cell at support@mentaura.example.
