Who May Access Your Information?

Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) boundaries.

1. You
Full personal access to your submissions and case journey.
2. Assigned Counsellor
Information needed for assigned psychological reviews.
3. Case / Support Officer
Approved case milestones and relief coordination.
4. District & State Admins
Aggregated statistical insights (no individual notes).
Internal staff notes and model vectors are strictly isolated from the victim portal.
